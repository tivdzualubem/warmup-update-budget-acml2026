# Execution provenance

The completion-flag conflict is explained without altering the historical flags. The returned master manifest and four dataset manifests are byte-identical to their pre-execution launcher versions. All 49 returned design/configuration/environment JSON files match that launcher. They describe the package before execution; the 240 per-job record groups supply the subsequent recorded outcomes.

`python audit_execution_provenance.py` independently checks the preserved launcher ZIP, returned results ZIP and all 1,499 lines of the separately supplied AG News log. It writes a per-run mapping, split and initialization checks, individual console-epoch matches, source-copy digests and a summary. All required inputs are inside this revision.

The current audit has no failures: 240 scientific record/status/manifest groups cover the exact expected matrix, with 180 test endpoints and 60 diagnostic records; 20 severity diagnostics remain separate. The audit checks 1,573 history rows, 55,535 trace rows and 277,675 LR fields. All seven initial fingerprints agree within each of 40 groups. The original launcher contains 224 matching scientific Python/configuration copies, representing 56 distinct files across four dataset copies.

The AG News console log is partial, not a complete study log. Its 635 printed epoch rows agree with returned histories at four-decimal precision and its 40 completion reasons agree. It also records two queue-handling failures and a later cross-dataset threshold lookup failure before the remaining phases. Those errors are preserved. Later per-job records support recorded completion, but do not identify the final launcher patch bytes or final complete orchestration history.

The returned `source_commit` and version/configuration fields are provenance declarations. Source compatibility, consistent digests and internally consistent records are not execution-time attestation of source bytes, original-host environment or a bit-exact original training rerun. The supplied 11 October student launcher hotfix now matches the Telegram-recorded SHA-256 exactly. Its pure patch function was applied to the original four wrapper texts in memory: it fixes tuple unwrapping and dataset scoping before threshold lookup, is idempotent, and produces syntactically valid wrappers. No launcher or training was executed. These reconstructed bytes explain the repair supplied to the professor, but final host application logs and executed wrapper bytes remain unverified. Complete final master status and other original phase console logs are also absent from the examined package.

Scratch validation split hashes and dataset manifests agree across the 60 jobs for each dataset. They identify recorded splits but do not supply original ordered indices or token-to-ID bytes. Matching initialization hashes similarly do not recover checkpoint tensors. The old-laptop search reported loadable raw dataset caches, but this does not independently reconstruct the exact October tokenized states.

Candidate-epoch weights, prediction-level validation evidence and complete paired states immediately after transition 49,999 were not recovered. The original scratch runner deletes temporary rolling resume/best files on normal completion; this is compatible with their absence, not proof that no separate backup exists. The earlier large checkpoint folder in the correspondence belongs to an April cohort.

Machine-readable evidence is in `evidence/execution_provenance/` and `tables/execution_provenance_*.csv`. Original files and flags are unchanged.
