# Revision rationale

The main manuscript and supplement incorporate the completed fixed-budget configured-peak pair. Its small observed final CE/accuracy contrast limits practical extrapolation from the historical LR=1.0 stress test. Both full curves, positive-LR budgets, training costs and single-seed limits are retained. Historical checkpoint selections, the AG News weighting uncertainty, WNUT misses and small pretrained effects are unchanged.

The new pair has independently checked predictions and retained full states. Historical missing candidate-epoch assets remain a distinct limitation; no historical checkpoint was reselected. The deferred full recovery extension was not performed.
